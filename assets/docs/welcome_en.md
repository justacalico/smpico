### Hi! This is SMPico

SMPico installs games and apps on PICO headsets from your PC — drop a file, it handles the rest. A few important things before you start.

#### Which internet resources it uses

- **GitHub** (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) — the PicoLand game catalog and covers;
- **Yandex Disk, Google Drive, SberDisk** — game downloads from catalog links (only when you press "Download");
- **YouTube and your browser** — only when you open trailers and links.

SMPico has **no** own server, ads, analytics or telemetry.

#### What data it stores

Everything stays **on this PC only**: settings (`HKCU\Software\SMPico`), install history, save backups, screenshots, recordings and reports (`C:\ProgramData\smpico_workspace`). A problem report is created only by your button and is never sent automatically.

#### Tooltips and help

- Hover over a button or icon to see what it does.
- The full guide is in **Settings → Help and reset → Guide** (book icon).

By pressing "Accept" you agree to the above. A first-launch mark is saved to the settings and this window will not appear again. You can reopen it in **Settings → Help and reset**.
